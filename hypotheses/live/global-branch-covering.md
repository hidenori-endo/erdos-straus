# Global branch-covering problem

- status: open / main research problem
- source turns: [022](../../会話ログ/022-assistant.md), [024](../../会話ログ/024-assistant.md), [032](../../会話ログ/032-assistant.md)

The exact reformulation says that a prime is solved when some admissible shift k makes at least one of the Type-I or Type-II targets land in the relevant divisor box. The unresolved semantic task is to prove that a hard prime cannot avoid both targets for every admissible shift.

The current reduction removes the upper Type-II region and leaves the Type-I-only corridor bounded by the Type-II estimate and the Type-I top-strip estimate.
