# Fixed-fraction H reduction

- status: rejected in general
- source: [Turn 034](../../会話ログ/034-assistant.md)

The expectation that every fixed-fraction upper corridor k/p > theta reduces to finitely many H values is false. Taking b = a + 2 and e = 3 gives k/p approaching a/(a+2) while H = 4d can grow without bound. Finite H behavior is confined to thinner top strips such as p-k = O(sqrt(p)).
