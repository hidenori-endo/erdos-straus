# Intermediate-center-fiber classification under H = Q

## Status

Computer-assisted classification of the parity-aware minimal-box / closure criterion under the necessary condition `H = Q`, where `H` is the subgroup generated by the mandatory seed primes and `Q = ker chi`.

Notation:

- `k == 3 (mod 4)` and `h in {1,121,169,289,361,529}`;
- `G=(Z/kZ)^x`, `chi=jacobi(.,k)`, `Q=ker chi`;
- `s=gcd(210,(h+k)/4)`, `H=<prime divisors of s mod k>`;
- `D=Div(s^2) mod k`, `P(x)={1,x,x^2}`;
- `d=gcd(k,840)`, with reduction `rho:G_k -> G_d` and kernel `K`.

Every seed prime is p-QR for a hard class, so `H subset Q`; hence `H >= Q` means `H=Q`.

## 1. Exact center fiber

The admissible centers are

`F = { p/4 mod k : p == h (mod 840), gcd(p,k)=1 } = c0 K`,

where `4 c0 == h (mod d)`. This gives three regimes:

- `|K|=1`: fixed center, `k=3,7,15,35`;
- `K=G`: full center, `d=1`;
- `1<|K|<|G|`: intermediate center fiber.

An intermediate fiber is either mixed (`chi|K` nontrivial) or monochromatic. In the latter case the hard class is square modulo every odd divisor of 840, so the fiber is QR-only.

## 2. Mixed fiber

For a one-NR compressed state `C=s r m`, with `r` NR and `m` QR, the guaranteed box is `D P(r) P(m)`. Put

`U_m = D {m^-1,1,m}`, `lambda_m=(4 s m)^-1`.

The exact E=1 condition on a mixed fiber is

`-m^-1 F_- subset U_m union lambda_m U_m`,

where `F_- = F intersect NR`. Counting gives `phi(k) <= 12 phi(d) |D|`; refining by the available seed primes gives the practical bound `phi(k) <= 2592`.

At quotient level, write `K_Q=K intersect Q` and `nu(a)=#{x in D : x == a (mod d)}`. The six translates can cover at most

`sum_z nu(ell z^-1)`, for `z in {mu^-1,1,mu,lambda mu^-1,lambda,lambda mu}`,

of the relevant `K_Q`-coset. In the exhaustive scan, 297 of the 332 mixed candidates fail this count. The remaining 35 class-pairs reduce to 23 structural rows; every row has an explicit `m=1` miss, with largest `k=335`.

## 3. QR-only fiber

Here `K subset Q`, so all centers are QR and non-pure states have even NR parity. It is enough to test the diagonal E=2 state `r1=r2=r`, with

`m = c / (s r^2)`, `B = D P(r)^2 P(m)`.

Since both targets are NR, only odd powers `r^1,r^3` can hit. With `g3=|G[3]|`, diagonal annihilation requires

`|Q| <= (6+3 g3)|D|`.

Under `H=Q` and an intermediate fiber, this implies `phi(k) <= 4698`, hence `k <= 44,142,408` using `phi(k)^2 >= k/2`. The global scan finds 26 QR-only candidates after the `H=Q` filter; 18 fail by counting and the remaining eight reduce to three rows:

| k | seed | d | c mod d | hard classes | explicit `(c,r,m)` |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 27 | 7 | 3 | 1 | 1,169 | `(1,2,1)` |
| 135 | 2 | 15 | 4 | 1,121,361 | `(64,127,68)` |
| 135 | 2 | 15 | 1 | 169,289,529 | `(1,7,62)` |

Direct substitution shows both targets are absent in each row. Thus no intermediate fiber survives.

## 4. Computation and consequence

The complete theoretical range scan reports:

```text
N=44142408 boundPairs=485 H=Q=358 maxHQk=8151
mixed=332 countFail=151 exactFail=181 qonly=26 countFail=18 exactFail=8 survivors=0 maxExact=0
```

Together with the fixed- and full-fiber results, this gives exactly 30 successful `(k,h)` pairs under `H=Q` for all `k`, not merely `k<=403`:

- fixed fiber: 20 pairs;
- full fiber: 10 pairs, `k=11,19,31,47,59`;
- intermediate fiber: 0 pairs.

A failed minimal-box state is an abstract closure witness; it does not by itself assert that an actual prime realizes that support pattern. Positive cases are range-free because the minimal box is contained in the true `Div(C_k^2)` box.
